Task 2: Problem Statement

Objective

DharaBot has an important delivery to make, fresh vegetables for a close friend of the farmers who lives deep in the hills. But the only way there is a narrow, twisting trail that winds between steep cliffs and towering mountains. The curves are sharp and tricky, where one wrong turn could send DharaBot wobbling off the path! Use the path dimensions to guide its wheels carefully, applying kinematics and wheel equations to steer and speed just right. Help DharaBot navigate the treacherous route and deliver the vegetables safely at the valley’s end!

The Theme for this task is “Actuators”.

We will be focusing on understanding Differential Drive robots and how they behave on sending different instructions to the two motors of the robot. We will try to understand this motion/behavior with the help of some mathematics/geometry.

The objective of Task 2 is to trace the path outlined on the arena and pass through the maximum possible number of waypoints as shown in the image below.

This has to be achieved only by sending the sequence of instructions to the motors with no additional feedback, i.e. sensing of the environment around the robot. We shall cover the topic of “Sensing and Feedback” in the upcoming Tasks.

Scoring

There are two parameter that will define successful completion of the task.

  1. Score: Number of Waypoints covered
    • The green colored sphere objects that appears on the path one after the other are called “waypoints”.
    • There are a total of 50 waypoints that will appear as and when the simulation starts up until the end.
    • The robot only needs to touch the waypoint to successfully capture the waypoint.
  2. Time Bonus: Based on Time of Run
    • The time remaining at the end of run is the time bonus that will be considered for total score. The greater the “Time remaining” the greater will be the Time Bonus.
    • When the simulation starts, a timer of 180 seconds would automatically start. The time would be stopped either when 180 seconds have elapsed or when all the waypoints have been reached.
    • Also, note that covering fewer waypoints can affect the “Time remaining” bonus.


$$ \text{total_score} = \left( \frac{\text{waypoints_reached}}{50} \times 100 \right) + \left( \frac{\text{remaining_time}}{180} \times 100 \times \frac{\text{waypoints_reached}}{50} \right) = \text{200} $$ $$ \text{Bonus 50 marks, if all waypoints_reached = 50} $$
