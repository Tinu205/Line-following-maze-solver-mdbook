# Task 5: PD / PID Control

**Duration:** 1 week

## Brief

Extend your Task 4 P controller into a PD controller (and optionally a full PID), using the [PID controller](../../Learning/Introduction%20to%20robotics/PID%20controller.md) page. The D term should visibly reduce the overshoot and oscillation your P controller had.

See [Putting It Together](../../Learning/Introduction%20to%20robotics/Putting%20It%20Together.md) for how the PID correction fits into the bot's full control loop.

See [Assignment](Assignment.md) for what to submit.
