## ROBOTS

<div style="text-align:center;">
    <img src="../../Assets/Images/robotics intro.jpg" width="250">
</div>

A robot is a machine designed to carry out tasks on its own (autonomously), partly on its own (semi-autonomously), or controlled remotely by a person (tele-operated).

- It can be programmed to perform specific actions or respond to various inputs, including sensory data from the environment.
- Robots come in various forms, from industrial robotic arms used in manufacturing to autonomous vacuum cleaners and humanoid robots.
- Using sensors, robots can notice their surroundings and react to them.
- Robots that work near people must be designed to be easy to use and safe.

## ROBOTICS

Robotics is the study and engineering field dedicated to designing, constructing, operating, and applying robots.

It is a multidisciplinary field that brings together expertise from various fields, including mechanical engineering, computer science, and electronics, to create versatile and efficient robotic systems.

<div style="text-align:center;">
    <img src="../../Assets/Images/Fields in robotics.png" width="250">
</div>

**It includes:**


- **Hardware (robotic components):** involves developing the physical parts (hardware) of robots, such as robotic arms and wheels.
- **Software (control systems and algorithms):** means creating computer programs (software) that act as the brains of the robots, guiding their actions.
- **Sensors and actuators together:** means combining sensors and actuators to make functional robotic systems that can sense their surroundings and perform tasks.

## Types of Robots

<div style="text-align:center;">
    <img src="../../Assets/Images/types of robots.jpg" width="250">
</div>

**AMRs (Autonomous Mobile Robots):** AMRs are self-navigating robots used for material handling and logistics, enhancing efficiency in warehouses by autonomously transporting goods.

**AGVs (Automated Guided Vehicles):** AGVs are wheeled robots guided by predefined paths or markers, commonly employed in manufacturing and logistics for automated material transport.

**Articulated Robots:** These robots have jointed arms, offering flexibility in movement for tasks like assembly and welding in manufacturing, thanks to their wide range of motion.

**Cobots (Collaborative Robots):** Cobots work safely alongside humans, aiding in tasks that require precision and flexibility, fostering human-robot collaboration in various industries.

**Humanoids:** Designed to resemble humans, humanoids are advanced robots capable of mimicking human movements and interactions, finding applications in research, entertainment, and assistance.

**Hybrids:** Combining features of different robot types, hybrids are versatile machines suitable for various tasks, adapting to specific needs by incorporating elements from AMRs, AGVs, and other robotic categories.

**Where does our bot fit?** A line follower is a small cousin of an AGV: a wheeled robot that follows a path marked on the floor.
