Welcome, young innovators, to the thrilling world of robotics, where curiosity transforms into creation! In this exciting journey, we'll cover the basics of robotics and understand how machines move, sense, and think.

The upcoming modules will guide us to:

- Explore the Sense-Think-Act paradigm, revealing how robots make decisions using sensors.
- Learn about the major robot components, from sensors and microcontrollers to motors and motor drivers.
- Learn the art of motion control and motor drivers, gaining the skills to bring your robotic creations to life.
- Step into the role of the controller, guiding your robot with Bang-Bang and PID control.
- Teach your robot to solve a maze using the LSRB rule, and put it all together.

The excitement peaks with an exhilarating challenge: design, build, and program your robot for a mission that will showcase your newfound expertise. Get ready for a hands-on exploration into the captivating world of microcontrollers and robotics, where each discovery sparks the thrill of creativity!
