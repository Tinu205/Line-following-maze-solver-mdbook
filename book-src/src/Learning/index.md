Over the next few weeks you are going to build a robot that drives itself.
 
Not a remote-controlled car but a bot that looks at a black line on the floor with its own sensors, works out where the line went, and steers to stay on it. By the last task it will find its way through a maze, using rules that you write yourself.
 
You will start by learning to write code, and everything after that is built on top of it.

Now let's start with understanding how to code.

## Your map for this section

Go through the four sections in this order:

| Step | Section | What you will get out of it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [C++](Cpp/index.md) | Write simple programs: variables, decisions, loops, functions and arrays. |
| 2 | [Introduction to Electronics](Introductions%20to%20Electronics/index.md) | Understand circuits and the parts your bot is made of. |
| 3 | [Introduction to Microcontrollers](Introduction%20to%20a%20Microcontroller/index.md) | Meet the Arduino Nano and program it to read sensors and drive outputs. |
| 4 | [Introduction to Robotics](Introduction%20to%20robotics/index.md) | Put it together: motors, control algorithms and maze-solving logic. |

You will also need some software for the later sections. Set it up with the [Arduino IDE Installation](../Software%20Setup/Arduino_ide.md) guide and the [Velxio Editor](../Software%20Setup/Velxio%20Editor.md) page.
